# Project status

Updated October 9, 2026.

## Goal

Build a private feed that periodically caches a large batch of Poshmark Suggested Items, removes unwanted brands and prices, and supports fast browsing without contacting Poshmark on every swipe. Product detail and purchase activity remain on Poshmark.

## Implemented

### Collection

- `collect.cjs` launches visible Chrome through Playwright and observes the normal personalized feed responses generated by page browsing.
- A run targets 200 unique listing cards and has bounded scroll, time, and idle limits.
- Listing IDs are deduplicated. Source order and observation timestamps are retained.
- The collector saves one cover image per listing and closes its temporary browser context.
- Poshmark credentials and cookies are not persisted by the collector.

### Filtering

- Asking prices above $150 are excluded. Exactly $150 remains eligible.
- The 53-brand blocklist is stored in `preferences.json`.
- Structured brand labels are preferred. Title matching is used when a listing has no usable brand.
- Mainline J.Crew and Banana Republic remain distinct from their blocked Factory labels.
- Unknown and vintage brands remain visible.
- Poshmark source order remains the default ranking.

### Local feed

- SQLite stores listing metadata and collection-run status.
- Cover images are cached under the ignored `data/images` directory.
- `server.cjs` serves the feed only on `127.0.0.1:4173`.
- The interface shows a single cover image and links each card to its Poshmark listing.

### Supabase

- Project URL: `https://umubcxkoxvzxucpwrwmm.supabase.co`
- Region: `us-east-1`
- Tables: `collection_batches`, `listings`, `user_listing_state`, and `filter_rules`
- Storage bucket: private `listing-images`, with a 5 MB object limit
- Every public-schema table has RLS enabled and owner-scoped authenticated policies.
- Anonymous users have no listing-table grant.
- The collector secret and owner UUID live only in ignored `.env.local`.
- `sync.cjs` uploads the existing local cache. `collect.cjs --sync` uploads after a new collection.

## Architecture

```text
Signed-in Poshmark feed
        |
        v
Local Playwright collector
        |
        +--> SQLite and local cover images --> local feed
        |
        +--> Supabase Postgres and Storage --> planned authenticated PWA
                                                    |
                                                    v
                                            Poshmark listing link
```

The Poshmark session remains on the collector computer. Supabase stores listing metadata and cached cover images. The browser frontend will use a publishable key and authenticated RLS access. The Supabase secret key remains limited to the collector.

## Known constraints

- The personalized feed endpoint and rendered selectors are undocumented and can change.
- Sustained unattended collection and account-blocking behavior remain untested.
- Poshmark's terms prohibit scraping and automated collection. Personal use does not remove account or access risk.
- Five listings in the first cache lacked a successfully cached cover image.
- The cloud frontend has not been connected to Supabase or deployed.
- Companion interactions do not update Poshmark's recommendation system.
